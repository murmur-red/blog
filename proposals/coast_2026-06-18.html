01 The observation

What I noticed

You're hiring a Manager of CS in a player-coach model alongside CSMs and an offshore CS Associate — a frontline team scaling fast right after the Series B. You've got Salesforce, HubSpot, Intercom, Stripe, and Mixpanel in place, but a stack that size usually means onboarding and reporting still run on manual handoffs rather than triggered workflows.

The business case

What that costs

When onboarding and QBRs are manual, each CSM loses 6-8 hours a week to repeatable work and account coverage caps out far below where it should. Post-Series B, that shows up two ways: retention slips in the first 90 days because handoffs are inconsistent, and you hire to cover volume that automation could absorb — a CSM hire is €70k+ a year against a six-week build.

02 The proposal

Here's what I'd build for Coast

  • Automated onboarding sequence triggered from Stripe signups and Intercom events, with CSM handoff tasks created in Salesforce
  • Lifecycle health scoring in Salesforce driven by Mixpanel product usage, flagging at-risk accounts before renewal
  • Standardized QBR reports auto-generated from Salesforce and Mixpanel data, ready for CSM review
  • Expansion-signal alerts routed to CSMs when usage thresholds in Mixpanel cross defined limits
  • Documented playbook so the new CS Manager can onboard CSMs into the automated process without rebuilding it
