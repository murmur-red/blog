I work in tech. I have opinions. Most of them are at least 60% correct.

This blog exists because I kept repeating the same half-formed thoughts to colleagues, and at some point it felt more efficient to write them down. The posts here are not rigorous, not peer-reviewed, and definitely not investment advice.

Topics that will recur: AI hype cycles, what actually matters in a career, why most software is bad, and occasionally something I found interesting enough to write 800 words about at 11pm.

You can find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/murmur).
